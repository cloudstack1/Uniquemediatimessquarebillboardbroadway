Digital vs Traditional Billboards: What Works Best in Times Square

Brands evaluating a Times Square Digital Billboard often ask how it compares to static formats. The short answer: digital wins on flexibility, frequency and targeting, while still delivering the same massive physical footprint.

With digital screens, your creative can rotate throughout the day, adapt messaging by daypart, and integrate with Times Square Programmatic Ads buying so you only pay for the impressions that matter most to your campaign goals.

Traditional large-format placements still have their place for long-running brand campaigns, but most of our clients choose digital for the control and measurability it provides.

Our team can help you decide which format — or combination of both — fits your objectives and budget.
